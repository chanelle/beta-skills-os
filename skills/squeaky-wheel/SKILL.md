# Squeaky Wheel

**Version:** 0.1.0 · **Maturity:** Specification; platform integration and test runs pending.

## Purpose
Turn unresolved complaints into documented, proportional escalation with deadlines, remedies, and verifiable follow-through.

## Trigger
Invoke when the user requests the workflow or a previously authorized task requires it. Casual mentions do not authorize external actions.

## Inputs
Service provider; desired remedy; chronology; prior communications; complaint identifiers; evidence; relevant response deadlines; user-approved escalation scope.

## Procedure
1. Reconstruct the complaint history; do not restart at a generic customer-service script.
2. Identify the exact failure, documented harm, desired remedy, and requested response date.
3. Map the appropriate escalation ladder for the jurisdiction and issue, using current verified sources.
4. Produce a concise evidence-backed demand and a redacted packet.
5. Choose the least burdensome effective channel; batch drafts and prepare follow-ups.
6. Send or file only within approved authority; never impersonate a lawyer, regulator, journalist, or complainant.
7. Track receipts and substantive responses, not just outbound messages.
8. Escalate proportionately when a deadline passes; prevent duplicate or harassing contacts.
9. Close only after the requested remedy is verified or the user chooses to stop.

## Output contract
Case summary; remedy; evidence index; escalation stage; submitted actions and confirmations; next deadline; decision required.

## Acceptance scenarios
- Three unanswered emails: consolidate the chronology and avoid resending identical demands.
- A regulator complaint is drafted but not filed: status remains prepared, not submitted.
- An agency responds without addressing the remedy: case remains open.
- A deadline is missed: generate a proportionate next step, subject to approval.

## Guardrails
Never invent facts, approvals, tool access, legal conclusions, or outcomes. Keep sensitive case data out of public repositories. Distinguish completed work, prepared work, and planned work. Do not assert that a background process exists without a configured runtime.
